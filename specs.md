## 1. Project name and overview

### Project name

NagrikLens

### Overview

**Overview & Main Purpose:**
NagrikLens is a neuro-symbolic GovTech platform designed to bridge the structural gap between complex government welfare policies, real-world citizen documents, and administrative remediation.

Instead of acting as another generic AI chatbot or static scheme directory, NagrikLens functions as an end-to-end casework engine across four connected stages:

1. **Discover (SchemeGraph):** Evaluates citizen eligibility across central and state welfare programs using a deterministic rules engine and conflict graph to identify legally stackable benefits and flag mutually exclusive restrictions.
2. **Verify (ClaimCraft):** Audits uploaded certificates, marksheets, and rejection notices using OCR and an Indic entity-reconciliation engine to catch spelling, honorific, transliteration, and validity discrepancies before submission.
3. **Resolve (CivicRoute):** Translates application rejections and document errors into verified administrative remediation pathways, providing step-by-step checklists and downloadable clarification drafts.
4. **Understand (PolicyMirror):** Analyzes newly issued government circulars and simulates rule changes across representative citizen personas to show how policy shifts alter eligibility in real time.

## 2. Problem statement

### Problem

Despite the widespread digitization of welfare schemes through Digital Public Infrastructure (DPI), millions of eligible citizens—especially students and low-income households—fail to access their rightful benefits due to four systemic bottlenecks at the administrative last mile:

1. **Opaque Eligibility & Hidden Exclusions:** Critical eligibility conditions and mutually exclusive clauses (e.g., barring concurrent central scholarships or restricting benefit-stacking) are buried in dense 20–40 page circulars. Existing portals evaluate schemes in isolation, leading citizens to inadvertently apply for conflicting schemes or miss out on combinable benefits.
2. **Pre-Submission Document Verification Gaps:** Government portals run automated cross-verification across disparate records (academic marksheets, Tehsildar-issued income/caste certificates, identity records). Because transliteration from Indic scripts to English varies across kiosks (e.g., *Md.* vs. *Mohammed*, *Km.* vs. *Kumari*), applications are frequently flagged as "Suspect Data" or rejected outright without clarifying whether the issue is a minor spelling variance or a fatal mismatch.
3. **Cryptic Rejections & Fragmented Redressal:** When applications fail, portals display terse status codes without actionable guidance. Citizens do not know whether to approach their Institute Nodal Officer, the District Welfare Officer, or an online portal like CPGRAMS or state IGRS, nor do they know how to draft an admissible clarification.
4. **Unannounced Policy Amendments:** Welfare departments regularly issue corrigenda and government orders that alter income ceilings, attendance thresholds, or mandatory certificate formats mid-cycle, leaving grassroots applicants unaware until after they spend resources applying under obsolete criteria.

**Why It Matters:**

Access to education and social protection should not depend on a citizen's ability to decipher bureaucratic legal prose or navigate fragmented nodal hierarchies. While portals have successfully digitized *submission*, the cognitive and procedural burden of *compliance, verification, and recovery* still rests entirely on the applicant. NagrikLens eliminates this friction by transforming unstructured policy text and raw document data into deterministic eligibility checks, pre-submission audits, and verified remediation pathways.

## 6. Processing rules and business logic

### Processing logic

The application transforms raw citizen inputs, document images, and regulatory text into structured casework through a **neuro-symbolic processing pipeline**. To eliminate hallucinations, all mathematical, eligibility, and procedural routing operations run on deterministic rule engines, while AI models are strictly constrained to multimodal extraction, semantic comparison, and natural language explanation.

---

### 1. Intake Sanitization & Profile Normalization

* **PII & Guardrail Interception:** Incoming text is scanned via regular expressions and validation filters to detect and discard forbidden sensitive identifiers (such as 12-digit Aadhaar sequences or complete bank account numbers).
* **Attribute Normalization:** Raw user inputs are mapped into an immutable `CitizenProfile` schema (e.g., standardizing income into integer INR, normalizing education level to standardized enums like `UG_TECHNICAL_DEGREE`, and cataloging category and domicile). Missing optional attributes are preserved as explicit `null` fields rather than guessed.

---

### 2. Deterministic Eligibility & Conflict Evaluation (SchemeGraph)

Instead of prompting an LLM to evaluate eligibility, the system executes an automated, rule-based predicate loop:

* **Predicate Execution:** The engine iterates through the target scheme catalog, evaluating boolean and relational expressions (`LESS_THAN_OR_EQUAL`, `EQUALS`, `IN_LIST`) against the `CitizenProfile`.
* **Pass:** All mandatory criteria satisfied $\rightarrow$ `Potentially Eligible`.
* **Fail:** One or more hard criteria violated (e.g., gender mismatch, income ceiling exceeded) $\rightarrow$ `Appears Ineligible` (with the failed predicate explicitly recorded).
* **Missing Data:** A required condition evaluates against a `null` field (e.g., orphan/martyr ward status unspecified) $\rightarrow$ `Additional Information Required` (triggering an explicit follow-up question).


* **Mutual-Exclusivity Graph Traversal:** Using an in-memory compatibility graph (NetworkX), the engine checks edges between all matched schemes:
* If Scheme A and Scheme B share a `MUTUALLY_EXCLUSIVE_WITH` edge (e.g., concurrent central maintenance allowances under AICTE and CSSS), the system flags a **Conflict Alert** preventing double-claim traps.
* If Scheme A and Scheme B share a `CO_CLAIMABLE_WITH` edge (e.g., state tuition fee reimbursement combined with an institutional merit award), the system groups them into a recommended **Stackable Benefit Package**.



---

### 3. Document Extraction & Entity Reconciliation (ClaimCraft)

Uploaded documents are processed through a multi-stage audit pipeline:

* **Vision/OCR Text Extraction:** Multimodal models and OCR extract structured fields: `applicant_name`, `father_name`, `date_of_birth`, `issuing_authority`, `serial_number`, and `issue_date`.
* **Validity Period Verification:** Certificate issue dates are calculated against the target scheme's financial year cutoffs (e.g., checking if a Tehsildar Income Certificate was issued within the statutory 3-year or current financial year window).
* **Entity Reconciliation Matrix:**
* **Honorific Stripping & Expansion:** Tokens like `Md.`, `Mohd`, `Mohammad`, `Km.`, `Kumari`, `Smt.`, and `Shri` are normalized to canonical roots.
* **Phonetic & String Similarity Scoring:** Names are evaluated using token sort ratios and Double Metaphone phonetic encoding.
* **Discrepancy Categorization:**
* Similarity $\ge 0.90$ with recognized honorific/vowel shift $\rightarrow$ `TRANSLITERATION_VARIATION`.
* Similarity $< 0.80$ or non-phonetic variance $\rightarrow$ `CRITICAL_MISMATCH`.




* **Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation Gate:** The system blocks automatic legal equivalence. It surfaces the discrepancy table, asks the applicant to confirm whether both documents belong to them, and designates the canonical identity anchor (e.g., the Class 10 High School marksheet).

---

### 4. Administrative Routing & Casework Synthesis (CivicRoute)

* **Deterministic Workflow Matching:** The system queries the administrative knowledge base matching `(scheme_id, detected_issue, jurisdiction)` to locate the statutory remediation pathway.
* **Two-Track Resolution Logic:**
* **Immediate Track (Portal/Scrutiny Level):** Routes the student to their Institute Nodal Officer (INO) and District Welfare Officer (DWO) with guidelines on submitting a self-declaration or joint scrutiny affidavit during the correction window.
* **Permanent Track (Issuing Authority Level):** Identifies the state e-District or Tehsildar rectification process required to re-issue the certificate with updated spelling matching the educational record.


* **Grounded Draft Generation:** A citation-locked template engine compiles a formal clarification letter addressed to the competent authority, incorporating the applicant's verified details, document serial numbers, and relevant departmental guidelines—watermarked prominently as:
`DRAFT FOR CITIZEN REVIEW — NOT AN OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT DOCUMENT`.

---

### 5. Policy Clause Diff & Scenario Simulation (PolicyMirror)

* **Structured Clause Diffing:** Ingests baseline policy text and amended government circulars (*Shasanadesh*), parsing modifications into structured rule deltas: `Old Rule` vs. `New Rule` vs. `What Changed`.
* **Persona Simulation Engine:** Runs the baseline and amended predicate sets across 4 standardized citizen profiles. It computes the delta:

$$\Delta \text{Status} = \text{Status}_{\text{new}} - \text{Status}_{\text{baseline}}$$



and highlights which newly introduced clause (e.g., mandatory 75% biometric attendance or altered income ceiling) caused a status transition from *Eligible* to *Ineligible*.

---

### 6. Action Report Aggregation

The engine compiles the complete analytical trace—eligible schemes, conflict warnings, document audit tables, confirmation logs, and step-by-step remediation checklists—into a unified, printable **Citizen Action Report (PDF)**.

## 9. Safety, privacy, constraints, and integrations

### Safety, privacy, constraints, integrations

### 1. What Must Be Protected (Safety & Privacy Protocols)

* **Strict Data Minimization & Identifier Redaction:**
* The system must never collect, store, or log sensitive government-issued identity numbers (such as Aadhaar numbers or full bank account details). Input sanitization filters and regex interceptors must actively block these patterns at the intake layer.
* In generated draft letters and casework reports, personal identifiers must appear only as user-controlled placeholders (e.g., `[Bank Account / IFSC Redacted]`, `[ID Number Redacted]`) to prevent accidental PII leakage.


* **Ephemeral Document Processing:**
* Uploaded documents (income certificates, marksheets, rejection notices) must be processed strictly within temporary, in-memory buffers during OCR extraction and entity matching.
* No uploaded image files or raw OCR text dumps should be written to permanent storage or application logs. File buffers must be purged immediately after field extraction or upon session termination.


* **Session Privacy & User Autonomy:**
* Casework sessions must remain client-scoped or tied to ephemeral session tokens in SQLite with a prominent **1-Click Delete Profile & Clear Data** control that purges all associated records instantly.


* **Identity Boundary Protection (Anti-False Identity Assumption):**
* The system must never automatically merge or legally equate two records with differing names (e.g., *Md. Arif Khan* vs. *Mohammed Arif Khan*). It must enforce a **Human-in-the-Loop Confirmation Gate**, treating variations strictly as phonetic/transliteration observations until the citizen explicitly confirms ownership.



---

### 2. What Must Be Avoided (Operational & Legal Boundaries)

* **No Automated Portal Submission or Credential Harvesting:**
* NagrikLens must never solicit portal passwords, store login credentials, or attempt automated form submissions that bypass official government CAPTCHAs, two-factor authentication, or verification safeguards. Final submission remains entirely in the citizen's hands.


* **No Unverified Legal or Notarization Claims:**
* The system must not present generated documents as legal affidavits, court-admissible instruments, or notarized paperwork. All outputs must carry prominent watermarks:
`DRAFT FOR CITIZEN REVIEW — NOT AN OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT DOCUMENT`.


* **No Probabilistic or Hallucinated Calculations:**
* The AI must never be allowed to calculate income thresholds, evaluate age criteria, or determine scheme compatibility through unconstrained text generation. All rule matching must be executed by a deterministic, code-level rules engine.


* **No False Guarantees of Scheme Sanction:**
* The application must never guarantee approval or benefit disbursement. Outputs must always use calibrated statuses: *Potentially Eligible*, *Additional Information Required*, *Appears Ineligible*, or *Needs Official Verification*.


* **No Unsubstantiated Statistical Claims:**
* System explanations and problem statements must avoid ungrounded generalizations (such as arbitrary percentage claims on rejection causes) and cite only documented portal rules, gazettes, and Citizen Charters.



---

### 3. Constraints (Technical & Feasibility Boundaries)

* **Scoped Jurisdictional Focus (MVP Boundary):**
* To ensure 100% rule correctness within a rapid build cycle, the active ruleset must remain strictly bounded to a single defined jurisdiction and sector: **Central Government + Uttar Pradesh Higher Education & Technical Scholarships**. Broad, unverified nationwide coverage must not be claimed.


* **Air-Gapped Demo Resilience (No Live Scraping Dependency):**
* Official government portals frequently experience downtime, Cloudflare blocks, or maintenance windows. The platform must not rely on live web scrapers during critical workflows or demonstrations. All regulatory guidelines must reside in a curated, versioned local knowledge base backed by pre-cached fixtures for sample documents.


* **File Upload Constraints:**
* Strict validation limits: Accepted formats restricted to PDF, PNG, and JPEG; maximum payload capped at 5 MB per file; non-document binaries and executables rejected with explicit client-side warnings.

### 4. What Must Be Supported (Integrations & Interoperability)

* **Multimodal Extraction & Indic Linguistics:**
* Support for extracting structured key-value pairs from low-resolution scans, photographed mobile uploads, and digital PDFs.

## 10. Acceptance criteria and test examples

### Acceptance criteria and test examples

The application is verified through quantifiable acceptance criteria mapped across each layer of the neuro-symbolic pipeline, validated by end-to-end deterministic test scenarios.

---

### 1. Concrete Acceptance Criteria by Module

#### A. Ingestion & Privacy Guardrails

* **Criteria 1.1 (PII Interception):** Any user input containing a 12-digit numeric sequence (pattern matching standard Indian identity numbers) or complete bank account credentials must be intercepted at the API gateway, returning an HTTP `422 Unprocessable Entity` with an explicit privacy warning. No forbidden identifier may be stored in SQLite, passed to LLMs, or recorded in application logs.
* **Criteria 1.2 (Transient Memory Assurance):** Uploaded files must be processed in temporary memory buffers. Calling `DELETE /api/v1/profile/session` must completely purge all cached OCR extractions and user profile attributes, returning a verified empty session state.

#### B. Scheme Discovery & Conflict Graph (SchemeGraph)

* **Criteria 2.1 (Deterministic Accuracy):** Eligibility statuses (`Potentially Eligible`, `Appears Ineligible`, `Additional Information Required`) must be 100% reproducible across 50 consecutive runs for identical profiles. Zero mathematical or relational evaluation may be delegated to an LLM.
* **Criteria 2.2 (Missing Attribute Handling):** When an optional attribute required by an active rule predicate is `null`, the engine must assign `Additional Information Required` and explicitly identify the missing field rather than guessing or defaulting to ineligible.
* **Criteria 2.3 (Conflict Graph Traversal):** If a student qualifies for two schemes connected by a `MUTUALLY_EXCLUSIVE_WITH` edge (e.g., Central Sector Scheme CSSS vs. AICTE Pragati), the system must flag a `CONFLICT_DETECTED` warning, blocking single-package recommendations.

#### C. Intelligent Document Auditor (ClaimCraft)

* **Criteria 3.1 (OCR Extraction Integrity):** Extracted certificate data must populate the target schema (`name`, `father_name`, `dob`, `income`, `issue_date`, `serial_no`).
* **Criteria 3.2 (Transliteration vs. Hard Mismatch Discrimination):**
* Minor spelling, vowel, or honorific shifts (*Md.* vs. *Mohammed*, *Km.* vs. *Kumari*) must yield a phonetic similarity score $\ge 0.90$ and trigger a `TRANSLITERATION_VARIATION` classification.
* Completely different identities (*Md. Arif Khan* vs. *Rohan Verma*) must yield a score $< 0.80$ and trigger a `CRITICAL_MISMATCH` classification.


* **Criteria 3.3 (Human-in-the-Loop Gate):** The remediation engine must refuse to generate clarification drafts until the user explicitly checks the confirmation prompt declaring that both documents belong to them.

#### D. Administrative Remediation (CivicRoute)

* **Criteria 4.1 (Grounded Authority Mapping):** A detected issue must map to the verified statutory authority (e.g., Institute Nodal Officer / District Welfare Officer for scrutiny discrepancies; Tehsildar / e-District for certificate re-issuance). No hallucinated department names or unverified portal URLs may be output.
* **Criteria 4.2 (Watermark Compliance):** Generated draft PDFs must display a non-removable banner across every page:
`DRAFT FOR CITIZEN REVIEW — NOT AN OFFICIAL GOVERNMENT DOCUMENT`.

#### E. Policy Diff & Simulation (PolicyMirror)

* **Criteria 5.1 (Clause Delta Accuracy):** Ingesting a modified circular must parse changes into discrete predicate diffs (`field`, `old_value`, `new_value`, `effective_date`).
* **Criteria 5.2 (Simulation Determinism):** Running amended rules across test personas must produce verified status shifts (e.g., an income ceiling drop from ₹2,50,000 to ₹2,00,000 must transition an applicant earning ₹2,20,000 from *Eligible* to *Ineligible* without altering unrelated personas).
